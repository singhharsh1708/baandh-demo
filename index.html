        What to trust
        
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          SPH vs Delft3D FM
          
      
          Two different numerical methods on one scenario: a finite-volume shallow-water solver on a grid (Delft3D FM) and meshless shallow-water SPH particles. Where they agree, the map is robust to the method; where they differ, it is not.

          How to read it
          
      
            
        
          • CSI (critical success index) = both wet / (either wet), over cells deeper than 0.1 m. 1 means identical extents.
            • 
        
          • POD and FAR take Delft3D FM as the reference, which is a choice, not ground truth.
            • 
        
          • Swipe shows Delft3D FM on the left and SWE-SPH on the right. Difference shows Delft3D FM minus SWE-SPH depth.
            • 
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          Near-field SPH at the breach
          
      
          DualSPHysics, built from source here, runs a vertical slice through the breach: 25 m of water released through the embankment. It checks the breach outflow physics near the dam, where a shallow-water model is least reliable.
          
      
          
      
          Lab check: Koshizuka and Oka (1996) dam break
          
      
          
      
          
      
          Breach section runs
          
      
          
      
          Discharge per metre of breach width, from the reservoir volume loss, averaged over the window before reflections return. Ritter (1892) is the analytic dam-break discharge for the same head.
